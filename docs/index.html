01 / The method

A Skeptic With a Test Plan.

Longshot works like an independent tester: curious about the intent, unconvinced by the presentation, and willing to change its mind.

It challenges designers and superusers when a workflow conflicts with the task. It also reports what works. A good review earns its conclusions; it does not fill a quota of complaints.

Evidence Before Opinion

Separate observed defects, plausible usability concerns, aesthetic preferences and missing evidence. Keep agent observations distinct from human research.

Useful Recommendations

Explain the obstacle, preserve necessary safeguards, and provide an acceptance test. “Make it intuitive” is not a fix you can verify.

A closer look / The protocol

Discover First.
Diagnose Second.

Knowing how the app works can hide how hard it is to use.

The First Encounter

Try the task through the interface, without source-code shortcuts or a designer's prescribed route. Record what consequential controls suggest before acting. Disclose prior knowledge when a fresh encounter is no longer possible.

The Diagnostic Pass

Then use authorized deeper evidence to reproduce and explain what happened. Completion with privileged help stays separate from unassisted discovery.

Real Conditions, Not Invented People

Choose relevant conditions: returning to a draft, comparing options, keyboard-only use, narrow screens, zoom, interruption or incomplete information. These are test conditions, not simulated demographics or human opinions.

Check What Happens After “Done”

Find the result. Return to it. Correct a mistake or recover work where authorized. Record completed, blocked, uncertain and untested states, plus any backtracking or help needed. A toast alone does not prove the job is finished.

02 / Put it to work

Test the Journey.
Not Just the Screen.

01

Before You Ship

Attempt onboarding, search, form completion or a core workflow. Find dead ends that a beautiful happy-path demo can miss.

02

When Work Gets Interrupted

Leave, return, cancel, retry. Check whether drafts, choices and context survive the routes people may need to take.

03

When “Done” Is Ambiguous

Distinguish a queued operation, a success message and a verified result. Investigate recovery without blindly resubmitting.

04

When the Default Path Excludes Someone

Exercise relevant keyboard, focus, zoom and responsive states. Record the limits of checks and identify where actual user research is needed.

05

When Everyone Agrees Too Quickly

Ask for an independent challenge to a designer's assumptions. Preserve useful expert complexity; question friction that serves no demonstrated purpose.

03 / How the loops work

Repeat to Learn.
Stop When the Evidence Ends.

1

Attempt

Choose a goal and discover the route before consulting implementation details.

2

Observe

Record expectations, outcomes and assistance. Keep each evidence source clear.

3

Challenge

Try an authorized alternative, interruption or competing explanation.

4

Verify

Check the result and relevant aftermath. Reproduce consequential findings within scope.

These are bounded review cycles carried out by your agent using its available tools. Longshot is an instruction skill, not a background service, a new browser engine or an automatic repair loop.

Stop at scope, access or action limits. Report blocked and uncertain states. If you later authorize a fix and retest, compare the same task conditions and check for regressions. No endless retries. No self-approved redesign.

04 / The deliverable

A Report You Can Act On.

COMPLETION

Did the task complete? Record assistance, prior knowledge and untested states.

REPLAY

Starting state → actions → expected and observed outcomes → screenshots or traces, when available.

JUDGMENT

Classification, severity, confidence and competing explanations

NEXT MOVE

The smallest useful change, safeguards to preserve, tradeoffs and a test that could disprove the recommendation.

A blocked task or lost work stays visible. An average score cannot make it disappear.

npx skills add jongos/Longshot --skill longshot-human-centered-ui

Or copy the skill folder into your agent's discoverable skills directory. Codex: ~/.codex/skills/.

Give It a Task

Use $longshot-human-centered-ui to test this demo app. A first-time visitor needs to prepare a draft using a required reference, then return later to finish it. Use the supplied synthetic account. Report completion, assistance needed, reproducible obstacles and acceptance tests. Do not modify the application.

Requires a host agent and appropriate browser or application tools. No separate evaluation service or companion skill is needed. If interaction is unavailable, Longshot labels its report as a limited evidence review.

07 / Behind the name

A Little Luck.
A Healthy Distrust of the Show.

Longshot is named for one of Jon Gosier's favorite X-Men characters. Created by Ann Nocenti and Art Adams, he debuted in Longshot #1 in 1985: an outsider with probability-bending luck, escaping a world ruled by the entertainment tyrant Mojo.

His connection to Dazzler is part of the comics, too. They become X-Men teammates and romantic partners, and their story takes them to Mojoworld. Later stories identify them as Shatterstar's parents, with a characteristically tangled time-travel twist.

The software connection is simpler: Dazzler helps shape the presentation; Longshot questions whether the experience holds up. The name is a personal tribute. Evidence, not luck, should carry the review.

08 / Honest limits

An Agent Tester.
Not a Human Study.

Longshot does not measure human confusion, satisfaction or task success merely by acting like a user. Its current validation includes small synthetic evidence exercises; broad rendered-task reliability remains unproven.

It does not certify accessibility, inspect private content without authorization, submit paid actions, or change your application just because you requested a review.

Human-grounded benchmark research is planned, not completed. The next evaluation should compare supported findings, missed consequential problems and recommendation quality against independently reviewed human evidence.
